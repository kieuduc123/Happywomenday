Dat bo anh theo tung nguoi vao thu muc:

public/gifts/<slug>/

Vi du:
public/gifts/hongnhung/1.jpg
public/gifts/hongnhung/2.jpg
public/gifts/hongnhung/3.jpg

Khi mo /hongnhung, he thong tu dong doc anh 1..N (jpg/jpeg/png/webp/gif/avif).

Neu can tuy bien text/nhac/chi dinh danh sach anh, tao them:
public/gifts/<slug>/config.json

Mau config nam o:
public/gifts/_template/config.json
